# Original example-site imagery

These four PNGs were created with the built-in `image_gen` tool for the fictional example websites. They are not photographs of real clients, products offered for sale, or an actual guesthouse. Original outputs were copied into this workspace without alteration. `manifest.json` records dimensions, byte counts, SHA-256 hashes and descriptive alt text. Each image was uploaded separately into its owning site's media library; the ordinary image-processing pipeline generated four sizes and reached `active` status. `../resources/media-bindings.json` describes the intended document bindings without embedding deployment-specific IDs.

- `fieldwork-studio.png`: Core homepage and studio hero.
- `slow-current-bench.png`: Journal homepage hero and The public bench article.
- `common-supply-collection.png`: Depot homepage collection hero. This is not a substitute for individual product photography.
- `aster-house-terrace.png`: Aster House homepage and house hero.

## Final generation prompts

### Fieldwork Studio

Original editorial photograph for Fieldwork Studio, a fictional independent website design studio. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. A quiet sunlit creative studio, a long pale birch worktable with deliberately arranged cream paper wireframe sketches, a charcoal notebook, graphite pencils and a few muted rust and navy color swatches. Two simple chairs, shelves with plain books and material samples in soft focus, tall north-facing window on right, calm off-white walls. Tangible thoughtful craft, organized but genuinely used, natural late-morning daylight, restrained warm-neutral tones. No computer screens, no readable words, logos or text, no people, no watermarks. Photorealistic magazine architectural still life, generous composed negative space, no decorative clutter.

### Slow Current

Original editorial photograph for Slow Current, a fictional independent journal, illustrating the essay The public bench. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. A beautifully ordinary weathered timber park bench with backrest beside a pale gravel walking path, shaded by a mature deciduous tree, facing a modest neighborhood green. Early autumn morning with warm side light, soft greens and gold leaves, quiet atmosphere, subtle real imperfections. Low human-eye camera angle, bench foreground and path receding naturally; calm story-driven composition suitable for a long-form magazine article. No people, signs, lettering, logos, watermark, exaggerated fantasy or dramatic postcard scenery. Authentic photorealistic documentary style.

### Common Supply

Create a refined editorial product photograph for Common Supply, a fictional shop for durable everyday objects. Landscape 3:2 still life on a warm ivory plaster tabletop: one chalk-white gently tapered 300ml stoneware mug with a comfortable handle, one folded natural oat flax linen kitchen cloth with visible open weave, and one small shallow rectangular oak desk tray with soft corners, arranged with generous negative space and natural asymmetry. Soft daylight from a large window at left, beautiful restrained shadows, tactile genuine materials, simple contemporary catalog photography, camera at a gentle three-quarter angle. No other objects, no branding, text, labels, logos, hands or watermark. Each object clearly recognizable with physical proportions. Photorealistic, understated neutral color.

### Aster House

Create a premium editorial architectural photograph for the fictional Aster House hospitality website. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, a modest single-story contemporary country guesthouse with pale warm limestone walls and natural oak framed floor-to-ceiling windows, a sheltered terrace, native grasses, a lone mature tree and distant foothills. Early morning side light, calm natural color, subtly weathered real materials. Restrained, authentic hospitality magazine art direction, not a luxury mansion. No people, no pool, no cars, no signage, no typography, no logos, no watermark. Photorealistic. Image must stand alone without text. Save generated output for use as an editable website media asset.
