# Aster House

A place-led field journal for mountain retreats, outdoor experiences and objects with lasting value. This is a complete pack built on the proven SDK behaviors; it owns its compositions and shared parts.

## Visual direction

- Warm limestone canvas, deep forest ink, vermillion for actions and navigation. Two presets: Limestone & forest and Dusk & ember. Every surface consumes theme tokens, with separate dark overrides.
- Cormorant Garamond display type, DM Sans body. Large cover headlines with a narrow measure, modest uppercase wayfinding, sharp corners and generous image space.
- The home cover uses the authored page title, excerpt, featured image and image caption. It yields to a CMS hero block when the author chooses one. Page blocks remain the content source.
- Shop objects use portrait images and restrained labels. Articles favor large photography and readable measures. Auth/member/plugin surfaces share the same pack-owned primitives.
- Movement is limited to focus, navigation and deliberate image hover; no essential information requires motion.

## Content contract for site authoring

Set a static homepage with a featured photograph and alt text, a short excerpt, and authored composition blocks. Supply menus through the per-template header/footer locations. Products, dates, prices, memberships, photographs and all business claims belong to site data, never the pack.

Recommended homepage narrative: a concise cover statement; an image/paragraph about the place; a live experience listing; a live product collection; recent journal writing; a newsletter or membership invitation. These are authoring suggestions, not built-in claims.

## Acceptance

Exercise all catalog surfaces, password/member gates, search, cart/checkout, courses, recipes/gallery/forms, support, auth and the member dashboard. Verify both presets and custom nested header/footer controls. Record screenshots on the actual staging site; component SSR and coverage checks do not replace browser acceptance.
