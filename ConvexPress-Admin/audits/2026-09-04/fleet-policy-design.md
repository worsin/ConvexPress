# C05 — fleet maintenance policies

One policy per environment explicitly enables full-storage backups, periodic identity/liveness health probes, and optional retention. Configuration is authorized by current site backup/connection capabilities (plus live-environment permission); each scheduled dispatch and health result revalidates the authorizing operator and active hierarchy. Authorization loss pauses the policy and creates an in-app incident. No external email/chat delivery is introduced.

A minute cron reads bounded due policies. Per-policy transactional dispatch advances due times and starts the existing durable backup workflow under its existing target lock. Scheduled operations/backups carry policy provenance. Busy targets defer; interrupted operations stay locked and surface an incident. Health leases prevent duplicate probes and ignore stale completion after edits. Health probes check bounded public identity/health responses without minting recurring site sessions. Incidents deduplicate by policy/kind; state transitions and policy activity are kept as paginated events.

Retention requires an explicit policy switch and applies only to that policy's verified manual-purpose backups. Keep the newest configured number and all recent backups. Skip deletion while any target operation is active; preserve any snapshot referenced by operation/pre-backup receipts, any artifact shared by another backup, and all snapshots while an unexpired handoff exists for the website. Delete storage transactionally with a deleted backup tombstone and a retention event. Historical receipts remain. Pagination bounds scans and does not treat truncated reads as absence.

The existing operations panel receives policy controls and paginated event/history access. Tests exercise actual mutations/queries with real Convex test storage and stubbed workflow/network boundaries; no provider actions are executed. Cases cover scope/parent disablement, revoked policy authors, duplicate cron dispatch, busy-target deferral, health leases/incidents, retention provenance/keep rules/active reference safety and pagination.
